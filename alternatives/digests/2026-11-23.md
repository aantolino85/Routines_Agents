# Alternatives Deal Desk — week ending November 23, 2026

*Window: Nov 17–23, 2026. Deduped against last week's log. Several scouts ran out of search budget, so fund closes, CRE values and secondary pricing may be under-covered this week.*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Partners Group investors vote to split its €6.6B flagship evergreen fund** into a portfolio that pays out cash as assets are sold and one that keeps compounding. Redemption requests ran over the 5% quarterly cap for two quarters in a row. This is the first big semi-liquid PE fund to split after hitting its gate.
- **Apollo and Goldman price a $44B SpaceX GPU financing**, upsized from about $40B. BlackRock, GSAM and KKR-advised accounts anchor it. It is the largest AI-hardware private credit deal so far.
- **Private credit stress shows up in the data:** Houlihan Lokey's Q3 default rate nearly doubled (1.5% size-weighted, from 0.8%), Fitch's US private credit default rate sits at 6.2%, and bank auctions of data center loans stalled on weak bids.

## 🏦 Wealth-channel products
- **Partners Group Global Value SICAV splits in two (Nov 23).** About 83% of votes cast approved splitting the €6.6B fund into a portfolio that pays out cash as assets are sold and one that keeps compounding. Redemption requests were about 9.8% in Q2 against a 5% cap, and payouts were prorated in Q2 and Q3. Partners Group plans a "material commitment" from its own balance sheet. [Citywire](https://citywire.com/selector/news/partners-group-to-restructure-6-6bn-flagship-evergreen-fund/a2499314) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-23/partners-group-shareholders-vote-to-split-key-fund-into-two)
  - *Why it matters:* clients now face an election, and this sets a template other gated evergreen PE funds could copy.
- **BlackRock/HPS seek SEC relief for exchange-traded interval funds (filed Nov 16, covered Nov 17).** These would be interval funds listed on an exchange and traded intraday. Monthly repurchases of at least 2% would go only to authorized participants redeeming in creation units. Not approved. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001086364/000119312526462163/d178417d40app.htm) · [Daily Upside](https://www.thedailyupside.com/advisor/industry-news/etfs-or-interval-funds-blackrock-asks-why-not-both/)
  - *Why it matters:* this would be a liquid wrapper for private assets. The question to ask is how wide the discount to NAV gets between repurchase windows.
- **LODAS Markets to run secondary auctions for Variant Alternative Income Fund (NICHX) (Nov 19).** Holders get an exit besides the quarterly repurchase. First auction expected January 2027. [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/lodas-markets-and-variant-investments-introduce-interval-fund-auctions/)
- **CAZ Investments and iCapital launch two Canadian access vehicles (Nov 17).** CAZ strategies (sports stakes, GP stakes) reach Canadian advisers through iCapital's white-label feeders. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/caz-investments-and-icapital-expand-partnership-in-canada-302941736.html)
- **Non-traded BDC liquidity.** Non-traded BDC redemptions reached $12.7B in 1H 2026. At a Wolfe Research summit, managers said they *hope* redemption requests fall below 5% of NAV by 1H 2027. That is a manager forecast with no data behind it. [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/alternatives/after-a-tough-year-are-private-credit-funds-and-bdcs-in-line-for-a-comeback-in-2027/269075)

## 📈 Market shifts
- **Private credit defaults are rising.** Houlihan Lokey's Q3 size-weighted default rate was 1.5% (0.8% in Q2), and 3.7% by count. 15.1% of borrowers cover less than 1.0x their interest, and 7% of loans are marked below 90. [ACI](https://alternativecreditinvestor.com/2026/11/19/private-credit-default-rate-nearly-doubles-in-q3/) Fitch's US private credit default rate is 6.2% (trailing 12 months), with 10.7% for borrowers under $25M EBITDA versus 2.4% above $100M. [Investment Executive](https://www.investmentexecutive.com/news/research-and-markets/u-s-private-credit-defaults-ease-in-october-fitch/)
- **Banks are winning back large loans.** 9fin says US direct-lending deal count fell 36% in Q3. Private credit's large-cap share dropped to 40% from 55%, and its spread premium over syndicated loans narrowed to about 220bp. [Connect Money](https://www.connectmoney.com/stories/banks-win-back-large-loans-as-private-credit-deal-count-falls-again/)
- **JPMorgan forecasts leveraged-loan defaults more than doubling to 4.5% in 2027 (reported).** About $862B matures in 2027–29, and about 46% of it is rated B3 or lower. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/jpmorgan-warns-leveraged-loan-defaults-set-to-double-in-2027) · [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/market/jpmorgan-warns-leveraged-loan-defaults-set-to-double-in-2027-11794949833068.html)
- **Data center debt is getting harder to sell.** ING's roughly $500M data center loan auction stalled on low bids. Debt on the Oracle-linked Project Jupiter was indicatively priced in the mid-80s (reported). Sovereigns are still bidding for platforms: GIC and Saudi Telecom are competing for a Pure DC stake. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-20/ing-s-500-million-data-center-loan-auction-stalls-on-low-bids) · [IREI](https://irei.com/news/gic-saudi-telecom-compete-for-stake-in-u-k-data-center-developer-pure-dc/)
- **Distributions are recovering.** Canoe's Q2 2026 data shows private funds paid out about $2 for every $1 called. Net cash returned to investors (excluding hedge funds) was a record $17.6B, and PE distributions have exceeded calls for five straight quarters. [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/alternatives/private-markets-hand-back-nearly-2-for-every-1-called-in-q2-2026/269090)
- **Hedge funds had their worst week since April 2025** in the AI selloff, losing more than 3% for the week and about 4% so far in November. Equity long/short net exposure is at multi-year lows. [Hedgeweek](https://hedgeweek.com/news/hedge-funds-suffer-worst-weekly-losses-since-april-2025-amid-ai-selloff) Balyasny closed its flagship to new money (reported). [Hedgeweek](https://hedgeweek.com/news/balyasny-closes-flagship-fund-to-new-capital) Maryland's $80.6B pension will exit its roughly $4B hedge fund book by 2028 and move the money to private credit and infrastructure. [Beinsure](https://beinsure.com/news/maryland-pension-fund-to-exit-4-bn-hedge-fund-portfolio-by-2028/)
- **Sports lending risk.** Apollo reportedly dropped a planned $2B refinancing for City Football Group after the Premier League ruling. [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/e2cfb9c4-a642-4ca9-9e78-32afc2549d9d)

Investors included one co-founder's former advisory clients. The SEC got an asset freeze, and SDNY and the CFTC brought parallel charges. These are allegations. **Due diligence flags**
- **Gated evergreen funds:** after Partners Group, ask every evergreen PE manager what happens if redemption requests stay above the cap for several quarters. Does it split, use a balance-sheet backstop, or prorate indefinitely?
- **Single-borrower and collateral concentration in private credit and ABF sleeves:** SpaceX GPUs, music catalogs, Oracle-linked data center loans. Ask for the top-10 exposures and how they are marked.
- **Operational red flags:** Backward Hat is the classic case of steady, outsized returns "verified" by an unknown auditor. Confirm independent administrators and auditors on any small private fund.

**Themes for client conversations**
- Private credit is getting more crowded and riskier at the same time. Spread premiums are compressing, banks are taking back large loans, and defaults are rising among smaller borrowers. Ask managers about borrower size, PIK and non-accruals.
- Distributions are finally flowing back to drawdown fund investors, which helps commitment pacing. Retail evergreen vehicles are moving the other way, with liquidity being rationed.
- AI exposure runs through every sleeve: hedge fund drawdowns, data center debt marks, GPU financings. Expect questions about AI concentration across a client's whole alternatives allocation.
- Product innovation keeps coming (exchange-traded interval funds, interval-fund auctions). Each one changes how liquidity works, and each needs a look at how it trades relative to NAV.
