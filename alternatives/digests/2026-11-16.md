# Alternatives Deal Desk — week ending November 16, 2026

*Window: Nov 10–16, 2026. First issue of this desk, so nothing below was deduped against earlier weeks.*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Non-traded BDCs are rationing liquidity.** BCRED filled about half of Q3 redemption requests, Ares Strategic Income Fund filled 38% of shares tendered, and Blue Owl Credit Income filled about 30% and paid in promissory notes. Goldman's BDC met every request.
- **Private markets are moving into model portfolios and target-date funds.** Victory Capital and Apollo filed for an interval fund for WestEnd models. SEI and Carlyle launched PRVTX with a $1,000 minimum. John Hancock and Great Gray are building target-date CITs that hold up to 20% in private markets.
- **Blackstone's listed data center REIT (BXDC) makes its first deal:** about $5B for 75% of AWS powered-shell joint ventures, at a 7.0% initial cap rate, with a single tenant on a triple-net leaseback.

## 📈 Market shifts
- **The Fed's November Financial Stability Report** says private credit defaults are at the upper end of their range and PIK use is heavy. Some perpetual non-traded BDCs saw fewer redemption requests, but they are still limiting redemptions and net flows remain negative. It also says hedge fund gross leverage hit a record. [Fed](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/financial-stability-report-20261113.pdf)
- **Invest Europe H1 data:** buyout fundraising rose 29% to €53.6B, while investment fell 35% and exits were the lowest for a first half since 2020. Fundraising for continuation vehicles fell 78%. [AltAssets](https://www.altassets.net/featured/european-private-equity-fundraising-rises-despite-35-investment-slump-as-exits-hit-six-year-low.html)
- **Goldman Sachs Alternatives LP survey:** 56% of LPs feel under-allocated to private credit, up from 43%. 84% of GP responses named wealth-channel or evergreen growth as the main theme, and more than half named rising defaults as the top risk. [ACI](https://alternativecreditinvestor.com/2026/11/10/private-credit-pushes-into-wealth-channel-as-investors-look-to-bulk-up-allocations/)
- **AI data center debt is meeting resistance.** Bloomberg reports that Blackstone's QTS cut an AI debt sale by more than half, to $300M (headline only; terms not seen). [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-13/blackstone-s-qts-is-cutting-ai-debt-sale-by-more-than-half)

Viking told investors it had been "overly conservative" and is moving toward more concentrated bets. [Biggo/Bloomberg](https://finance.biggo.com/news/2c9a4c371b6fe471) · [Hedgeweek](https://hedgeweek.com/news/viking-global-urges-bigger-bets-as-long-short-performance-falls-short)

[Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/private-creditroundup-lenders-consolidate-seek-scale-as-earnings-pressure-builds-5025455)

## ⚖️ Regulation & policy
- **SEC FY2026 enforcement results:** 348 actions and about $1.1B in monetary relief. Private fund cases focused on pre-IPO SPV feeders, undisclosed fees, custody failures and fair value on principal loan sales (Madison Capital Funding). [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-113-sec-announces-enforcement-annual-results-fiscal-year-2026)
- **FSB and Bank of England:** Andrew Bailey warned that private credit's links to banks and insurers could amplify a shock. Interim results of the BoE's private markets stress exercise, which includes Apollo, Ares, Blackstone and KKR, are due this year, with a final report in H1 2027. The FSB is also planning a consultation on hedge fund leverage in sovereign bond basis trades. [ACI](https://alternativecreditinvestor.com/2026/11/13/boes-bailey-interconnectedness-could-see-private-credit-pose-risk-to-financial-stability/) · [Hedgeweek](https://hedgeweek.com/news/global-regulators-target-hedge-fund-leverage-in-sovereign-bond-markets)
- **FINRA** asked the SEC to let industry governors hold a majority of its board, ending the public-member majority in place since 1996. FINRA writes the sales-practice rules for private placements, non-traded REITs and BDCs. [WealthManagement.com](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/regulation-compliance/wall-street-regulator-finra-moves-to-give-industry-bigger-role)
- **SEC executive-pay proposal:** it includes a Form S-11/N-2 timing change that would make it easier for non-traded REITs and BDCs to keep offerings open. There is a 60-day comment period. [AltsWire](https://altswire.com/sec-pay-proposal-would-ease-registration-timing-for-reits-bdcs/)
- **Coming up:** the DOL's final safe-harbor rule for alternatives in 401(k)s has been under OMB review since Nov 5. Comments on the SEC's September "retailization" package (interval fund changes, accredited investor pathways) are due around late November or early December. [PlanAdviser](https://www.planadviser.com/dol-submits-dc-alts-safe-harbor-rule-for-final-white-house-review/)

## 🎯 Angle for the RIA
**Manager watch**
- **Blackstone:** BXDC's ~$5B AWS deal, BCRED filling half of redemption requests, BREIT impairments with positive flows, and the reported cut to the QTS debt sale. Clients holding both BREIT and BXDC are doubled up on Blackstone data centers, and BXDC's new assets lease to one tenant.
- **Apollo:** the Victory interval fund puts Apollo strategies into adviser models without Apollo being the fund's adviser. ADS is in net outflow. Ask how the underlying Apollo strategies are chosen and what fees they carry.
- **KKR:** closed the USI exit ($3.3B of after-tax proceeds to KKR), closed the RWE offshore wind JV, and plans a GP-led secondaries strategy with Arctos. Whether drawdown fund LPs get distributions depends on which funds held USI.
- **Ares:** a €3.3B credit continuation vehicle, and ASIF filled only 38% of tendered shares. Holders of older Ares credit funds may be offered a choice to sell or roll.
- **Blue Owl:** OCIC paid redemptions in promissory notes. Ask about the notes' terms and timing.
- **Goldman Sachs AM:** its BDC met every redemption request. Co-CEO David Miller leaves Dec 31.
- **Carlyle:** its first wealth product with SEI (PRVTX).
- **Partners Group:** a new ELTIF channel, while its US evergreen fund is still working through a redemption backlog.
- **StepStone:** removed its co-COO structure (minor) and is a proposed infrastructure manager in the John Hancock CITs.
- **Brookfield, TPG, Hamilton Lane, iCapital, CAIS:** nothing big found this week. Search coverage was partial.

**Due diligence flags**
- **Promissory-note redemptions and pro-rata fills:** at OCIC, BCRED and ASIF, find out what clients actually received, when, and in what form.
- **Distributions above NII:** BCRED's nine-month distributions exceeded NII while non-accruals rose. Check distribution coverage and PIK share for every BDC on your platform.
- **Fee layering in "one-ticket" interval funds:** PRVTX and the Victory/Apollo fund sit on top of underlying fund fees. Look past the 0.75% headline to the total expense ratio, and check how much each fund relies on its partner manager's own products.

**Themes for client conversations**
- "Semi-liquid" means gated when demand spikes. Use this quarter's fill rates (GS 100%, BCRED ~50%, ASIF 38%, OCIC ~30%) to reset expectations.
- Private markets are reaching model portfolios and 401(k) target-date funds. Plan-sponsor clients will ask about this as the DOL rule nears.
- The AI and data center boom is now in listed REITs, non-traded REITs and infrastructure funds. Map each client's total exposure across vehicles.
- The secondaries market is maturing (credit CVs, CVs of companies already in CVs, real estate LP discounts). Expect more offers to sell or roll in clients' older drawdown funds.
