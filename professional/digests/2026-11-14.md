# Energy & Data Center Deal Desk — week ending Nov 14, 2026
*Window: Nov 7–14. The Nov 13 issue already covered most of Nov 6–13, so this issue lists only items that are new or that that run missed. It is also the first run with the standalone-storage and Enel-and-peers buckets.*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Data center credit is filling up: QTS cut a private placement from about $800M to about $300M (reported).** At the same time, Nscale's amended IPO filing says it needs at least $30B more to build out its Anthropic contract.
- **Sempra closed the sale of 45% of Sempra Infrastructure Partners to a KKR-led group** (the deal was announced at $10B). It's the utility playbook in action: sell infrastructure stakes to pay for regulated capex.
- **Exus Renewables NA raised a $345M holdco loan led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives.** Private credit keeps filling the holdco gap for mid-size US IPPs.

## 📈 Market shifts
- **Signs that lenders have had enough data center debt:** the QTS cutback, Nscale's ≥$30B funding gap, and Fermi asking a hyperscaler to prepay before signing a lease all point the same way. Combined with a 10-year yield above 5% (last issue), pricing for data center paper is moving in favor of lenders.
- **X-energy Q3:** about 11.5 GWe pipeline, $1.8B liquidity and no debt. Fluor was picked for Energy Northwest's Cascade project. The NRC granted the Dow / X-energy Seadrift construction permit on 11/09: 4 × 80 MWe Xe-100, the first US advanced reactor approved for an industrial site, with Dow's investment decision due in 2027. [X-energy 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002088896/000119312526456633/xe-ex99_1.htm) · [GlobeNewswire](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/09/3399007/0/en/dow-x-energy-proposed-smr-project-receives-historic-construction-permit-for-nuclear-powered-industrial-site.html)

## ⚖️ Policy, legal & regulatory
- **The Senate voted 73–25 to take up the bipartisan permitting bill** (11/09; vehicle H.R. 2347). It would limit permit revocations, give FERC a larger role in transmission siting and make new data centers pay their own energy costs. After the midterms, TD Cowen cut its odds of enactment from ~40% to ~25%. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/permitting-bill-advances-in-senate-but-its-future-is-uncertain-after-democ/874250/)
- **DOE's financing office (the former LPO) closed its first oil and gas production loan:** $480M to Arena Energy for 22 Gulf shelf platforms (11/10). [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/node/4861373)
- **About 400 onshore wind projects are waiting on Pentagon siting review, per court filings** (reported 11/12; plaintiffs' reply due 11/17). This puts tax credit timing and tax equity conditions precedent at risk. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/around-400-onshore-wind-projects-await-dod-action-official-says-in-court/874521/)
- **New York utilities (led by ConEd) propose owning up to 1 GW a year of renewables**, which would break with the state's no-utility-ownership model. A PSC white paper is expected later this year. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/coned-says-resource-adequacy-environmental-compliance-drive-planning/874511/)
- **Oregon PUC approves a separate rate class for Pacific Power's data centers of 20 MW or more** (11/13), effective Jan 1, 2027. They will pay full cost of service under long-term contracts. [KATU](https://katu.com/news/local/oregon-plan-requires-future-large-data-centers-to-pay-full-electricity-costs-pacific-power-costs-budget-business-politics-customers-utilities)

## 🎯 Angle for You
**Enel & peers**
- **Enel 9M results (11/12):** US renewables were a bright spot (higher output and better PPA prices). The US fleet is expected to top 13 GW by year-end, and the ~1.5 GW of brownfield deals signed group-wide, mostly in the US, are largely complete. Management assumed 5.5% on the debt for those deals. For Enel, this confirms the US is still its main market for buying operating assets. The next signal on US appetite comes at the February 2027 Capital Markets Day. *The figures come from AI-assisted transcripts; check them against Enel's release.* [OilPrice](https://oilprice.com/Company-News/Enel-Profit-Rises-6-as-Spain-and-Americas-Offset-Italy-Weakness.html)
- **NextEra and Avangrid both made NYPA's 1 GW+ nuclear shortlist** (11/12). Peers are moving toward firm 24/7 supply for Northeast offtakers. Enel's answer has to be storage-firmed hybrid PPAs. [NYPA](https://www.nypa.gov/News/Press-Releases/2026/20261112-nuclear)
- **On watch:**
  - Virginia SCC hearings on NextEra–Dominion start 11/17 (staff recommended rejecting the deal as filed).
  - AES take-private by GIP/EQT and EDF power solutions NA's sale to KKR could both close any week.

**Themes**
- **Data center debt is getting harder to place, which helps advisers.** With QTS shrinking a deal and Nscale needing ≥$30B, sponsors will need more creative structures: prepayments like Fermi's, holdco and preferred equity, ABS. This would make a good LinkedIn post.
- **Holdco and back-leverage for mid-size IPPs.** Exus–Goldman is the template. Peers with heavy construction pipelines and limited balance sheet are likely outreach targets.
- **Korean and Gulf strategics keep buying US projects** (Hyundai/OCI, Masdar/Terra-Gen). Buy-side advisory on construction financing and tax equity follows each purchase. Clairemont will need both from 2027.
- **Onshore wind risk from the Pentagon review:** sponsors with turbines exposed to the AMC/PTC deadline will need help re-cutting tax equity conditions precedent and PPA delivery dates.
