# Energy & Data Center Deal Desk — week ending Nov 13, 2026
*Window: Nov 6–13. The Nov 12 test digest already covered Nov 5–12, so this issue leaves out everything in it and focuses on items that run missed or that are new since then.*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Anthropic reportedly pays $3.4B for OnePWR's 1.7 GW of Caterpillar gas generators.** Equipment queue slots are now assets that change hands, at about $2M/MW.
- **Fervo signs a 396 MW, 15-year geothermal PPA with Google**, with an option for about 600 MW more. Its contracted backlog rises to about $11.9B, and GeoBlocks 4–11 will need a large amount of project debt.
- **Rates shock: the 10-year Treasury topped 5% after a hot October CPI report**, with a December Fed hike being priced in (reported). This hits deals that were sized on lower rates.

The gas works out to about $5,700/kW, another data point that new combined-cycle plants have become very expensive. Plants targeted to be operating by 2031; sites and regulatory path not disclosed. ## 📈 Market shifts
- **Rates:** the 10-year rose above 5% after a hot October CPI report (11/10) and was reported at about 5.15% after PPI on 11/13. A December hike is largely priced in. Some of the selling has been tied to worries about debt-funded AI issuance. *These levels come from press reports; check them against H.15.* [Invezz](https://invezz.com/uk/news/2026/11/10/10-year-treasury-yield-tops-5percent-after-hotter-cpi-data-increases-rate-hike-odds/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html)
- **SMR cost risk:** OPG says the first Darlington BWRX-300 will miss its Oct 2030 start date and its C$7.7B Unit 1 budget, with a revised estimate due by mid-2027 (11/11). That hits the completion-risk case for every BWRX-300 follower project, including TVA's Clinch River. [WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/opg-to-revise-timeline-and-cost-of-first-smr-unit)
- **EIA STEO:** about 72 GW of solar and 20 GW of wind over 2026–27, batteries reaching 91.7 GW by end-2027, and electric-sector gas prices forecast to fall 19% in 2027 (11/10). [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/as-coal-burn-declines-rising-power-demand-will-be-primarily-met-by-renewab/874315/)

## ⚖️ Policy, legal & regulatory
- **ISO-NE files its large-load interconnection proposal at FERC** (11/13). It has three tiers: firm, where the customer must bring its own new capacity and fund upgrades; interim non-firm; and permanent non-firm. Non-firm load is re-dispatched at $250/MWh and excluded from the capacity requirement. Target start is June 1, 2027. This bears directly on the Kraft Sutton proposal. [ISO-NE](https://isonewswire.com/2026/11/13/iso-ne-files-proposal-with-ferc-to-address-large-loads)
- **NYPA shortlists 10 developers for its 1 GW+ upstate nuclear program** (11/12): Avangrid, Elementl, NextEra, Oppenheimer, The Nuclear Company and Turquoise on both pathways, plus Blue Energy, Holtec, TerraPower and X-energy for SMRs. Host site and partner to be chosen "in the coming months." [NYPA](https://www.nypa.gov/News/Press-Releases/2026/20261112-nuclear)
- **Ninth Circuit splits California's climate disclosure laws** (11/09). SB 253 Scope 1–2 reporting goes ahead; Scope 3 and SB 261 were found likely unconstitutional. [Foley](https://www.foley.com/es/insights/publications/2026/11/california-climate-disclosure-laws-ninth-circuit-ruling-sb-253-scope-1-2-disclosures-move-ahead-sb-253-scope-3-disclosures-and-sb-261-expected-to-be-enjoined/)

## 🎯 Angle for Angelo
- **Equipment-slot and off-grid gas plays need capital.** OnePWR had to sell its queue position because it couldn't finance it, and STAK (3 GW in Alaska, no tenant or financing) is in a similar spot. Sponsors holding turbine or genset slots without balance sheet are natural clients for structured equity or monetization advice.
- **Holdco and pre-construction data center credit is the gap private credit is filling.** Rowan's Apterra-led term loan plus LC line is a model to pitch to mid-size developers like Integra, the Kraft site, and the Malaysia JDA co-investor search.
- **Rates above 5% will reopen deals that were sized on lower rates.** Expect re-sizing, hedge restructuring and sponsors looking at tax equity or credit-transfer mixes again. This would make a good LinkedIn post topic, alongside Constellation's Peak+ pricing ($20–50 above the PTC floor) against $110–130/MWh new gas.
- **Geothermal and nuclear new-build need financing.** Fervo's next 8 GeoBlocks and the 10 NYPA shortlist developers, especially the smaller ones such as Turquoise, Oppenheimer and Blue Energy, will need structuring help, and OPG's overrun makes completion-risk solutions part of the pitch.
