# Energy & Data Center Deal Desk — week ending Nov 23, 2026
*Window: Nov 16–23. It was a light week for large US renewables and gas M&A. Storage and data center credit carried most of the news.*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Enel Green Power North America agrees to buy energyRe's ~317 MWdc South Carolina solar portfolio.** energyRe stays on as construction manager through COD, and closing is expected in Q1 2027. It is another near-built US solar purchase for Enel.
- **EQT Transition Infrastructure agrees to buy Flatiron Energy from Hull Street Energy.** Flatiron has a 24 GWh Eastern US battery portfolio, 6 GWh of it under long-term contracts. Terms were not disclosed. It is the biggest US storage platform sale of the quarter.
- **Data center credit is splitting in two.** HSBC reportedly sold $300M of the $18B Stargate New Mexico construction loan in the mid-80s, and a $488M Malaysia data center syndication was paused. Meanwhile SpaceX priced $32B of GPU-backed debt and Blue Owl marketed AAA CMBS at +195bp. Stabilized, lease-backed paper is getting tighter spreads; construction risk is being sold off at a discount.

## 🏢 Data centers & power
- **Oracle says it has fully funded its OpenAI build-out and that this was its last capital raise of the cycle (11/20).** This follows the Nov 2 deal of ~$10B of stock plus $10B of notes at 7.000–8.650% coupons. Taking a big repeat issuer out of the IG supply pipeline should help spreads. [Securities.io](https://www.securities.io/oracle-secures-full-funding-for-openai-data-center-expansion/)
- **Banks are taking credit risk out of data center loans.**
  - HSBC sold part of its Stargate loan at a discount (table above).
  - Citi and JPMorgan reportedly added a US chip-export compliance default clause to Bain's Bridge Data Centres loan, which could be upsized to $6.6B (11/18). [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/citi-and-jpmorgan-reportedly-add-chip-curbs-clause-to-bain-data-centre-loan)
  - Syndication of Actis-backed Epoch Digital's $488M Malaysia loan, for ByteDance capacity, was put on hold (reported 11/16–18). [The Tech Capital](https://thetechcapital.com/actis-epoch-488-million-malaysian-loan-syndication-delayed/)
- **Ohio approves 2,150 MW of gas plants dedicated to data centers (11/19):** PowerConneX / EdgeConneX in Ashville (800 MW) and Advanced Power's Fairfield Energy Center (1,350 MW) for Vantage. Both will need construction financing. [Signal Cincinnati](https://signalcincinnati.org/2026/11/19/ohio-approves-two-large-power-plants-exclusively-for-data-centers/)
- **Data center bond spreads are tightening even as banks sell loans.** PIMCO expects AI data center bond spreads to tighten as issuance slows. Amazon data center bonds trade ~146bp over Amazon corporates, down from ~180bp in September. AI-related ABS has reached $28.6B this year, part of a post-GFC record for ABS issuance (reported 11/17–20). [Debt Serious (citing Bloomberg)](https://debtserious.substack.com/p/weekender-21-november-2026)
- **Late catch-up:** PPL and Blackstone (Invitium) signed a non-binding 2 GW LOI on 11/11 to power QTS in Salem Township, PA. It resurfaced this week. [Blackridge Research](https://www.blackridgeresearch.com/news-releases/ppls-advanced-stage-pennsylvania-data-center-pipeline-reaches-35-3-gw-with-2-gw-qts-deal-agreed-in-the-us)

## ⚖️ Policy, legal & regulatory
- **Permitting reform: the Senate invoked cloture 70–27 on the S. 5653 (BAAJA) substitute (11/16).**
  - Environmental assessments would be due in 1 year and EISs in 2, with a 150-day limit on lawsuits.
  - FERC could permit interstate lines of 230 kV or more directly.
  - Data centers would pay the full cost of the grid upgrades they need.
  - Analysts cut their odds of House passage in the lame duck to 20–25%, and the Dec 11 funding deadline competes for floor time. Don't underwrite permitting relief into 2027 schedules yet. [HNGN](https://hngn.com/articles/273848/20261116/senate-advances-permitting-bill-70-27-jeffries-opposes-it.htm) · [Planetizen](https://www.planetizen.com/node/138897)
- **DOE offered Grid United a conditional loan of up to $2.8B for the 1.8 GW Wyoming Intertie, an SPP–WECC HVDC link (11/17).** The loan runs 30 years at 80% loan-to-cost and can be assigned to utilities that buy capacity. No capacity customers are named yet. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-28-billion-loan-strengthen-grid-reliability-and-help-lower) · [POWER](https://www.powermag.com/doe-offers-2-8b-conditional-loan-for-1-8-gw-wyoming-link-between-eastern-and-western-grids/)
- **FERC's Nov 19 meeting had no co-location or large-load rule.**
  - NIPSCO may buy energy and capacity at market-based rates from its affiliate NiSource Generation to serve new load. Other utilities could copy this affiliate-generation model.
  - FERC acted on MVP and Sabine Pass Stage V items, upheld Kammer Juniata transmission incentives, and narrowed "deemed denial" of rehearing (RM26-13). [FERC](https://ferc.gov/news-events/news/summaries-november-2026-commission-meeting)
- **RTOs filed large-load interconnection reforms in response to FERC's show-cause orders (filed 11/13; SPP due 11/20).**
  - PJM proposes a one-year "Fast Energization Process" that studies large loads and generation together.
  - ISO-NE would require large loads to bring their own new capacity.
  - CAISO and MISO offer non-firm or flexible service options. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/pjm-rto-large-load-interconnection-ferc-show-cause/874872/)
- **North Carolina approved a Duke Energy data center rate class for loads over 50 MW (11/18).** It requires 10–15-year minimum contracts and early-termination fees, plus a "stranded generation charge" that requires collateral for the remaining life of new gas turbines. That materially raises the credit support data center developers must post. [WFDD](https://www.wfdd.org/2026-11-18/nc-regulators-approve-new-rates-for-data-centers-in-rate-hike-plan)
- **NextEra–Dominion (~$67B): Virginia SCC hearings began 11/17 and are running past Thanksgiving.**
  - Amazon wants approval conditioned on reform of Dominion's large-load queue (cluster studies, bring-your-own capacity).
  - Google is challenging Dominion's minimum demand charge.
  - The companies have accepted 101 of 117 staff / AG conditions but rejected a rate freeze and a jobs guarantee. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/nextera-dominion-merger-virginia-scc-hearing/875046/) · [Axios Richmond](https://www.axios.com/local/richmond/2026/11/17/dominion-nextera-virginia-scc-hearings-power-bills-jobs) · [12 On Your Side](https://www.12onyourside.com/2026/11/19/scc-merger-testimony-expected-run-into-next-week/)
- **New York released its Advanced Nuclear Master Plan with a 5 GW target (11/18).** NYPA is pursuing at least 1 GW, and its shortlist includes NextEra and Avangrid. No funding mechanism has been set, which leaves room for advisors on the financing structure. [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-11-20/article-8595/new-yorks-5gw-nuclear-master-plan/)
- **DOE's Section 202(c) emergency orders continue:** Talen's Wagner Unit 4 in Maryland must keep running through Dec 31 (11/18). [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-secretary-acts-protect-mid-atlantic-grid-0)

## 🎯 Angle for You
**Enel & peers**
- **Enel (11/20):** EGPNA agreed to buy energyRe's ~317 MWdc South Carolina solar portfolio, closing Q1 2027. It continues Enel NA's 2026 strategy of buying near-built US solar rather than developing it, alongside the 270 MW portfolio that closed in October. For Enel, this means more construction-to-COD execution risk sitting with the seller-manager, and continued appetite for brownfield deals in the Southeast. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/energyre-sells-a-portfolio-of-south-carolina-solar-projects-totaling-317-mwdc-to-enel-green-power-north-america-302947038.html)
- **AES (11/17):** with final FERC approval, the GIP / EQT take-private closes ~Dec 2. A privately owned AES Clean Energy can bid harder on hyperscaler PPAs and brownfield M&A, but expect it to recycle assets after closing, which could create portfolios for Enel to buy.
- **RWE Americas (11/18):** CEO Andrew Flanagan leaves at year-end, and Ingmar Ritzenhofen becomes interim CEO on Jan 1. RWE reaffirms €17B of US investment. A leadership transition may slow RWE's decisions in contested PPA and M&A processes, an opening for Enel. [RWE](https://www.rwe.com/en/press/rwe-ag/2026-11-18-rwe-americas-announces-leadership-changes/)
- **NextEra–Dominion:** disputed rate freezes and ring-fencing could limit how much NextEra can use Dominion's balance sheet in Virginia data center supply. That helps independent suppliers like Enel. The SCC must rule by about Jan 11.
- **EDF power solutions** bought the 250 MW Holloway BESS in Australia. This is outside the US, so there is no direct read-through for Enel NA.

**Themes**
- **Storage capital is abundant at the platform level and in bank debt** (EQT/Flatiron, Kansai/Prospect, esVolta's full MUFG stack). Developers with contracted PJM or Northeast storage can run competitive sell-downs. The opportunity is advising mid-size developers on a minority sale versus a platform exit.
- **Data center construction risk is being priced at a discount** (HSBC's Stargate sale in the mid-80s, the Epoch pause, chip-export default clauses). Lease-backed takeouts are tight (Blue Owl AAA at +195bp). Private credit buyers of discounted bank loans and sponsors that need bridge-to-CMBS structuring are clear outreach targets. Worth a LinkedIn post on the gap between the two.
- **Large-load tariffs now come with collateral requirements** (NC's stranded generation charge, ISO-NE's bring-your-own capacity rule). Data center developers will need LC and surety capacity and contract structuring. This is a growing advisory niche.
- **Dedicated gas for data centers is getting permits** (Ohio, 2.15 GW). Those projects will need construction debt and equity in 2027, and AMPERA-type SPACs show capital chasing behind-the-meter power.
