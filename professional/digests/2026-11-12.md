## 🏢 Data centers & power
- **Google, Wyoming: $17B campus powered by 702 MW of Bloom fuel cells with carbon capture**, contracted through AEP (11/05). A utility is supplying onsite gas power instead of the hyperscaler waiting for the grid. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/wyoming-data-center/)
- **NTT DATA: 900 MW of Bloom fuel cells** as the main power source for its data centers worldwide (11/05). [NTT DATA](https://www.nttdata.com/en-us/news/2026/ntt-data-announces-agreement-with-bloom-energy)
- **Venture Global is pitching off-grid gas power for hyperscalers**: 5 GW by 2031 and 10 GW by 2034, using its turbine slots (11/10). No customers have signed yet, so offtake and financing work will be needed. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/venture-global-worlds-largest-gas-exporter-power-data-centers-hyperscalers/)
- **SpaceX:** a $24B gas plant and compute site at Starbase, TX (11/09), and confirmed as developer of a proposed 6 GW campus in Cochise County, AZ (11/10). [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/11/10/texas-spacex-data-center-power-plant/) · [KJZZ](https://www.kjzz.org/fronteras-desk/2026-11-10/spacex-is-behind-proposed-data-center-in-cochise-county)

- **CoreWeave Q3:** $132.5B backlog, 4.6 GW contracted, about $10.3B of net debt raised in the quarter, and 2026 capex guidance of $37–39B (11/10). [CoreWeave](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Reports-Strong-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx)

## 📈 Market shifts
- **PJM capacity market design:** Exelon asked FERC to move PJM to "prompt" auctions, run two months before delivery, from 2030/31. Invenergy is challenging MISO's exclusion of merchant HVDC lines (5 GW Grain Belt) from its capacity market. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/exelon-invenergy-ferc-complaint-pjm-miso-capacity-market-/873845/)
- **New York utility ownership:** ConEd and other NY utilities proposed jointly owning up to 1 GW/yr of land-based renewables (11/12). That would put utilities in competition with IPPs. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/coned-says-resource-adequacy-environmental-compliance-drive-planning/874511/)
- **Election-day markets:** after the election, the S&P fell about 4.4% on 11/05 while utilities held up. [Heatmap](https://heatmap.news/daily/the-anti-utility-election)

## ⚖️ Policy, legal & regulatory
- **Permitting bill:** the Senate advanced it 73–25 (11/09). It includes a FERC "public interest" standard for transmission and data center cost-allocation rules, but TD Cowen now puts passage odds at about 25% after the midterms. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/permitting-bill-advances-in-senate-but-its-future-is-uncertain-after-democ/874250/)
- **Midterms:** Democrats took the Georgia PSC (3–2, the first time since 1995) and both open Arizona Corporation Commission seats. Voters backed data center restrictions in 40 of 41 local measures. [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/110626-factbox-democratic-gains-data-center-backlash-set-new-course-for-us-energy-policy)
- **Michigan Prop 2** bars utility campaign contributions, with more than 80% of the vote; it faces a legal challenge. [Heatmap](https://heatmap.news/daily/the-anti-utility-election)
- **Wind and DOD:** about 400 onshore wind projects are stuck in DOD review, with 8 staff to handle them, according to a court filing reported 11/12. That puts schedule and tax credit timing at risk. [Utility Dive](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/around-400-onshore-wind-projects-await-dod-action-official-says-in-court/874521/)
- **NRC construction permit for X-energy:** four Xe-100 reactors (320 MWe) at Dow's Texas site (11/10), the first advanced-reactor approval at an industrial site. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/x-energy-gets-green-light-to-build-its-first-small-modular-nuclear-reactors/)
- **DOE loan to Arena Energy:** its financing office closed a $480M loan for Gulf of Mexico platform upgrades (11/10). It's another sign the office now lends to fossil projects. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/node/4861373)
- **Tax credits after the election:** analysts expect remaining credits to be protected but none restored, with FEOC rules and tariffs staying in place. [PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/democratic-sweep-in-us-midterms-brings-reprieve-for-solar-but-federal-gridlock-persists/)

## 🎯 Angle for Angelo
- **Merchant gas needs structured capital.** Hallador (Turtle Creek term-out and offtake) and Venture Global's data center power push both need contracted offtake and financing. Hallador explicitly says it's open to other capital.
- **Behind-the-meter power for data centers is a real asset class now.** Fuel cells (Google, NTT) and onsite gas (SpaceX, Venture Global) need project finance structures that differ from grid-tied PPAs.
- **Regulatory risk just went up for utility-backed large-load deals** (Georgia, Arizona, Michigan, local data center bans). Expect lenders to scrutinize large-load cost allocation in diligence. This could make a good LinkedIn post topic.
- **The ING loan auction (bids due 11/19)** will set a secondary-market price for data center credit. Worth watching if you're advising any private credit clients.
