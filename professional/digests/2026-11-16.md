# Energy & Data Center Deal Desk — week ending Nov 16, 2026
*Window: Nov 9–16. The Nov 12, 13 and 14 issues already covered most of Nov 6–14, so this issue lists only items that are new or that those runs missed. It was a slow week for new transactions, so the issue is short. Searches hit their limit before the Nov 14–16 sweep was finished (see the note at the end).*

**Top 3 this week:**
- **Blackstone's listed BXDC buys 75% of two AWS data center JVs for about $5B at a 7.0% cap rate**, with AWS leasing everything back triple-net for 12 years (up to 32). This sets a public benchmark for hyperscaler sale-leasebacks.
- **The Fed's Financial Stability Report flags "circular" data center financing.** Hyperscaler debt issuance is above $680B through October, and about $3T of leases and backstops sit off balance sheet. Combined with the QTS cutback, lender diligence on data center paper is tightening.
- **Clearway sets 2027 guidance and a roughly $4.1B capital plan to 2030**, including about $2.1B of refinancing, new drop-downs and a hyperscaler LOI for its Wyoming Wilcox complex. A direct Enel peer is moving into data center power.

## 🏢 Data centers & power
- **CoreWeave is moving away from high-yield bonds toward debt backed by contracts, chips and prepayments** (reported 11/13). The biggest neocloud borrower is going the ABS / GPU-collateral route, which opens the door for structured lenders. Terms are behind a paywall. [DigiTimes](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20261112PD248/financing-data-center-expansion-capacity-demand.html)
- **Valar Atomics gets a Carbon County, Utah permit for 40–120 microreactors next to data centers** (11/12). It's the first local land-use approval at this scale for clustered microreactors, with gas as bridge and backup power. NRC licensing is still needed. [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/11/13/valar-atomics-carbon-county-utah-nuclear-reactors-data-centers-permit)
- **Coming up:** bids are due 11/19 on a roughly $500M portfolio of data center loans with Vantage, CyrusOne and Stargate New Mexico exposure. The clearing price will be a useful read on data center credit stress. [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/a-500-million-pile-of-data-center-loans-just-hit-the-auction-block/)

## 📈 Market shifts
- **Fed Financial Stability Report (11/13):** hyperscaler debt issuance above $680B through October (versus $130B in all of 2023), and about $3T of future leases and backstop guarantees. The Fed warns that circular leases, guarantees and equity between hyperscalers, chipmakers and neoclouds could produce losses bigger than balance sheets suggest. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/fed-says-its-monitoring-the-data-center-build-out-for-risks-to-the-financial-system-224116947.html)
- **PJM's market monitor expects the December 2029/30 auction to clear at the $325/MW-day cap again**, adding $5–10B of data-center-driven costs, and recommends postponing it until the reliability backstop can run (11/12–13). [WITF](https://www.witf.org/2026/11/13/electricity-grid-watchdog-warns-of-higher-prices-after-delayed-pjm-market-reforms/)
- **India's 10 GWh grid-scale cell manufacturing tender drew 22 bids** (Reliance, Amara Raja, Exide, JSW Neo, Waaree, ACME) (11/13). Non-Chinese cell capacity is slowly building, which matters for FEOC-compliant supply over time. [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/11/13/indias-grid-scale-battery-cell-tender-draws-22-bids/)

## ⚖️ Policy, legal & regulatory
- **The permitting bill cleared a second Senate vote, 62–21, to begin consideration** (11/10), after the 73–25 cloture vote. A final Senate vote is expected this week, then the House in the lame duck. The Nov 7 substitute drops the 20 MW threshold for data centers paying full transmission cost and adds 25–50% cost recovery if a permit is revoked in bad faith. [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/senate-permitting-bill-bolsters-data-center-provisions-moves-full-vote) · [WLJ](https://www.wlj.net/senate-advances-bill-overhauling-federal-permitting-rules/)
- **Voters approved 40 of 41 local ballot measures restricting data centers** (PV Tech analysis, 11/13). Siting backlash is now a demand risk for solar pipelines tied to hyperscalers. [PV Tech](https://www.pv-tech.org/us-midterms-data-centre-backlash-could-slow-solars-ai-boom/)
- **Coming up this week:**
  - Virginia SCC hearings on NextEra–Dominion start 11/17.
  - FERC open meeting and the CPUC vote on the PG&E–Google 250 MW deal, both 11/19.
  - CAISO's large-load filing at FERC was due 11/16 (not confirmed filed).
  - The Section 232 polysilicon tariff takes effect 12/4.

## 🎯 Angle for You
**Enel & peers**
- **Enel:** no new North America releases in the window. The 9M results (11/12, covered last issue) added detail: 2–3 GW of deals at binding-offer stage and about 5 GW in negotiation group-wide, with US PPA renewals pricing about $17/MWh above the portfolio average. For Enel NA, expect more brownfield M&A and repricing of offtake. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/enel-9m-2026-slides-networks-drive-8-eps-growth-investment-surges-93CH-5022923)
- **Clearway** (11/09) has a funded plan to 2031 and a hyperscaler LOI for up to 3.4 GW at Wilcox, WY (per the call). It raises the bar for Enel NA on data center offtake and will compete for Western and Midwest brownfield assets.
- **NextEra–Dominion** (reported 11/12–13): five Virginia House Democrats urged the SCC to reject the deal as proposed; ~2,400 comments filed. Hearings start 11/17 and the SCC decision is due by Jan 11, 2027. Delay or tough conditions leave more room for Enel in PJM and the Southeast. [ad-hoc-news (reported)](https://ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/nextera-energy-aktie-abgeordnete-fordern-stopp-der-dominion-fusion/70506115)

**Themes**
- **Hyperscaler sale-leasebacks are now a listed asset class.** BXDC's 7% cap rate on AWS shells gives sponsors a pricing reference, and the mortgage debt behind it is a new financing lane. Worth a LinkedIn post.
- **Data center credit is moving to structured deals:** CoreWeave's contract- and chip-backed loans, the Fed's warning and the $500M loan auction all point toward ABS, prepayment-backed and insured structures, which is advisory work.
- **Yieldco drop-downs at 10–11% CAFD yields still work** (Clearway). Sponsors with operating portfolios and a refinancing wall in 2028–32 are outreach targets.
