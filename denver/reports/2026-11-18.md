# Denver Weekend — Fri Nov 20–Sun Nov 22, 2026

**TL;DR:** Journey's farewell tour hits Ball Arena Sunday. For kids, it's Bluey's Big Play at the Buell on Saturday. Book now: Bluey tickets, and the Denver Zoo free-day timed tickets for Sunday (walk-ups get turned away).

## 🎸 Live music & nights out (top 4)
1. **Journey, Final Frontier Tour (An Evening With)** — Ball Arena, Denver · Sun Nov 22, 7:30pm · from ~$67 · Classic rock, and a farewell arena tour · [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/journey-ball-arena-20261122)
2. **22nd Annual Last Waltz Revisited, feat. Polytoxic & The Denver Horns** — Boulder Theater, Boulder · Sat Nov 21, doors 7pm, show 8pm · GA, all ages · A tribute to The Band's *Last Waltz* in its 50th-anniversary year · [Grateful Web](https://www.gratefulweb.com/?p=678485)
3. **Trans-Siberian Orchestra, "The Ghosts of Christmas Eve"** — Ball Arena, Denver · Sat Nov 21, 3pm & 7:30pm · resale from ~$90 · Symphonic rock with Savatage roots, big guitars and a big light show · [RateYourSeats](https://www.rateyourseats.com/tickets/events/transsiberian-orchestra-tickets-denver-ball-arena-november-21-2026-7536285)
4. **The Unlikely Candidates + Elk Darling (KTCL Presents)** — Moon Room at Summit, Denver · Sun Nov 22, 7pm · from $21 · Alt rock; it clashes with Journey · [Ones To Watch](https://onestowatch.com/live/denver-weekend-november-20-2026/)

Comedy alternative: **Mo Amer** at Comedy Works Downtown on Fri Nov 20 (7:15 & 9:30pm, $35, 21+). Friday is the only night not sold out. [Comedy Works](https://comedyworks.com/comedians/mo-amer)

**Heads up:** Big Head Todd and the Monsters, with Isaac Slade of The Fray, play the Fillmore on Sat Nov 28 at 6:30pm, from $78. It's a hometown 90s-rock holiday show. [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/big-head-todd-and-the-monsters-the-fillmore-auditorium-20261128)

## 🧒 With the kids (top 5)
1. **Bluey's Big Play** — Buell Theatre (DCPA), Denver · Sat Nov 21, 5pm Family Night (also listed for Sun Nov 22) · ages 2+, $24–$148 plus fees, and everyone needs a ticket · [denvercenter.org](https://www.denvercenter.org/tickets-events/blueys-big-play/)
2. **Roald Dahl's Willy Wonka (Fort Collins Children's Theatre)** — Lincoln Center, Fort Collins · Fri 7pm (ASL), Sat 11am & 4pm, Sun 2pm · kids $24–$29, adults $29–$35 · [lctix.com](https://www.lctix.com/shows-tickets/willy-wonka/)
3. **Denver Christkindlmarket opening night** — Tivoli Quad, Auraria, Denver · opens Fri Nov 20 at 6pm, with a drone show nightly at 7pm · free; carousel and s'mores · [Denver7](https://www.denver7.com/about/community-affairs/denver-christkindlmarket-returns-to-the-tivoli-quad-for-its-26th-year)
4. **Denver Zoo Community Free Day** — Denver Zoo, City Park, Denver · Sun Nov 22, the last free day of 2026 · free, but timed tickets must be booked online · [denverzoo.org](https://denverzoo.org/free-days/)
5. **Discovery Days Art** — Longmont Museum, Longmont · Thu–Sat Nov 19–21, 9:15am–2:15pm · ages 2–6 with a caregiver · [longmontcolorado.gov](https://longmontcolorado.gov/event/discovery-days-art-8/2026-11-21/)

Next week: Denver Zoo Lights opens Nov 23, and NoCo Winter Wonderland (Fort Collins) opens Nov 26. Nothing kid-specific could be confirmed in Loveland this weekend.
