# Denver Weekend — Fri Nov 20–Sun Nov 22, 2026

**TL;DR:** Journey plays Ball Arena on Sunday. With the kids, Blossoms of Light opens Friday at the Botanic Gardens. Buy tickets now for Last Waltz Revisited in Boulder (it sells out every year) and for timed entry to Blossoms of Light.

## 🎸 Live music & nights out (top 5)
1. **Journey: Final Frontier Tour (An Evening With)**: Ball Arena, Denver · Sun Nov 22, 7:30pm · from $67 · Classic rock arena headliner, no opener · [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/journey-ball-arena-20261122) / [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/journey-final-frontier-tour-an-evening-denver-colorado-11-22-2026/event/1E0064A4E2714602)
2. **Last Waltz Revisited: 50 Years of The Band**: Boulder Theater, Boulder · Sat Nov 21, doors 7pm, show 8pm · general admission · Tribute to The Band's Last Waltz. It sells out every year, so buy soon · [Grateful Web](https://www.gratefulweb.com/?p=678485) / [tickets](https://tixr.com/e/203853)
3. **Vertical Horizon**: Ophelia's Electric Soapbox, Denver · Thu Nov 19, 8:45pm · from $94 · 90s/2000s alt rock ("Everything You Want"), the best weeknight pick · [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/vertical-horizon-ophelias-electric-soapbox-20261119) / [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/breakthrough-presents-vertical-horizon-denver-colorado-11-19-2026/event/1E006512E3864980)
4. **The Unlikely Candidates (KTCL presents)** with Elk Darling: Moon Room at Summit, Denver · Sun Nov 22, 7pm · from $27 · Alternative rock with a radio-rock sound · [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/the-unlikely-candidates-the-moon-room-at-summit-music-hall-20261122) / [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/ktcl-presents-the-unlikely-candidates-denver-colorado-11-22-2026/event/1E006547C879CA7E)
5. **Mo Amer (stand-up)**: Comedy Works Downtown, Denver · Fri Nov 20 9:30pm or Sat Nov 21 9:15pm (the other shows are sold out) · $35 GA, 21+ · The non-music pick: the star of Netflix's "Mo" · [Comedy Works](https://comedyworks.com/comedians/mo-amer)

**Heads up:** Big Head Todd and the Monsters, with Isaac Slade (The Fray), play the Fillmore Auditorium on Sat Nov 28, 6:30pm, from $78. [JamBase](https://www.jambase.com/show/big-head-todd-and-the-monsters-the-fillmore-auditorium-20261128)

## 🧒 With the kids (top 5)
1. **Blossoms of Light (opening weekend)**: Denver Botanic Gardens (York St), Denver · Opens Fri Nov 20, nightly 4:30–9pm · All ages, $19–45 (demand pricing), free for kids 2 and under · Timed tickets are sold only on the Gardens' site · [Botanic Gardens](https://www.botanicgardens.org/events/special-events/blossoms-light)
2. **Downtown Loveland Festival of Lights**: 4th St & Foundry Plaza, Loveland · Fri Nov 20, 5–8pm, tree lighting 7:30pm · Free, all ages · Live music, a holiday market and carriage rides · [Visit Fort Collins](https://www.visitftcollins.com/noco-holiday-light-trail/)
3. **Willy Wonka (Fort Collins Children's Theatre)**: The Lincoln Center, Fort Collins · Fri Nov 20–Sun Nov 22 · Family musical. Showtimes and prices weren't online, so check the ticket page · [Lincoln Center tickets](https://tickets.lctix.com/eventperformances.asp?evt=2307)
4. **Switch on the Holidays**: Pearl Street Mall & Civic Area, Boulder · Sun Nov 22, 11am–7pm · Free, all ages · Cookie crawl at 11am, lights on at 5pm, free Santa visits · [Kids Out and About](https://denver.kidsoutandabout.com/content/switch-holidays-4)
5. **ICE! featuring "The Nightmare Before Christmas"**: Gaylord Rockies, Aurora · Opens Fri Nov 20, 9am–9pm · All ages · Ice sculptures and ice slides, kept at 9°F, so dress warm · Buy ahead · [Kids Out and About](https://denver.kidsoutandabout.com/content/ice-featuring-disney-tim-burton%E2%80%99s-nightmare-christmas) / [Gaylord](https://christmasatgaylordrockies.com/ice)

Also opening: Trail of Lights at Chatfield Farms in Littleton (Sat Nov 21) and the Denver Christkindlmarket (Fri Nov 20). Zoo Lights doesn't open until Nov 23.
