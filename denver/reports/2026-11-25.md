# Denver Weekend — Fri Nov 27–Sun Nov 29, 2026

**TL;DR:** It's Thanksgiving weekend. The top show is Big Head Todd & The Monsters at the Fillmore on Saturday, and the Last Waltz Revisited is tonight (Wed). For the kids, Grand Illumination at Union Station on Friday is free. Book Zoo Lights by date, and buy Boulder Nutcracker tickets only from the CU/Macky box office.

## 🎸 Live music & nights out (top 5)
1. **Big Head Todd & The Monsters + Isaac Slade (solo)** — Fillmore Auditorium, Denver · Sat Nov 28, 6:30 pm · $78–$149 · 90s rock from a Colorado band; Slade (The Fray) adds a 2000s set · [link](https://www.jambase.com/show/big-head-todd-and-the-monsters-the-fillmore-auditorium-20261128)
2. **22nd Annual Last Waltz Revisited ft. Polytoxic** — Fillmore Auditorium, Denver · **Wed Nov 25 (tonight)**, doors 7 pm · price not listed · Yearly Thanksgiving-eve classic rock tribute to The Band's Last Waltz · [link](https://app.discotech.me/events/38273432-22nd-annual-last-waltz-revisited-feat-polytoxic-at-fillmore-auditorium)
3. **Mr. Majestyk's 8-Track Revival** — Oriental Theater, Denver · Sat Nov 28, ~7:30 pm · price TBA (last year $25–$175) · 70s rock covers (Zeppelin, ELO, Alice Cooper) with a string section · [link](https://www.songkick.com/venues/8688-oriental-theater)
4. **Senses Fail — "Still Searching" 20th anniversary** — Summit, Denver · Sat Nov 28, 6 pm · price not listed · 2000s rock, played album-in-full; heavier than Muse or Panic! · [link](https://app.discotech.me/events/38228965-senses-fail-presents-still-searching-at-summit)
5. **An Evening with Firefall** — The Armory, Fort Collins · Fri Nov 27, 7 pm · price not listed · 70s soft/classic rock · One resale site lists it as unavailable, so confirm with the box office before driving · [link](https://detour.songkick.com/venues/4455927-armory)

**Heads up:** Matchbox Twenty + The Wallflowers play Red Rocks Sat May 22, 2027. Tickets are on sale now · [link](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/matchbox-twenty-morrison-tickets-on-sale-for-red-rocks-concert/)

## 🧒 With the kids (top 5)
1. **Grand Illumination** — Denver Union Station, Denver · Fri Nov 27, 4–8 pm (tree lights at 6:10) · all ages, free · Las Posadas procession with mariachi, Santa, and Elsa & Sven · [link](https://denverunionstation.com/events/grand-illumination)
2. **The Nutcracker Suite (1-hour, kid-friendly)** — Macky Auditorium, Boulder · Sun Nov 29, 1 pm (full-length version Fri/Sun 4 pm) · all ages · Pre-show storytime and cast meet-and-greet · Buy only through the CU/Macky box office · [link](https://www.boulderballet.org/the-nutcracker-2026)
3. **Rocky Mountain Train Show, Holiday Edition** — The Ranch (Pedersen Toyota Center), Loveland · Sat Nov 28 9–5, Sun Nov 29 9–3 · all ages · Trains kids can run, story time, Santa photos; one ticket covers both days · [link](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/rockymountaintrainshow/2420070)
4. **Santa arrives in Old Town Square** — Old Town, Fort Collins · Fri Nov 27, 11:30 am · all ages · Skating rink and Santa's Workshop nearby; holiday artisan market Fri–Sat at the Senior Center · [link](https://www.visitftcollins.com/blog/stories/post/things-to-do-in-and-around-fort-collins-over-thanksgiving-week/)
5. **Zoo Lights** — Denver Zoo, Denver · Fri–Sat 4:30–10 pm, Sun 4:30–9 pm · $29.95 adult / $22.95 youth (Sun cheaper: $25.95 / $18.95), under 2 free · Tickets are for a set date; buy online · [link](https://denverzoo.org/events/zoo-lights/)

**Thanksgiving morning (Thu):** free Gobble Gobble Kids Race in Old Town Fort Collins (~8:45 am, [link](https://runsignup.com/Race/Info/CO/FortCollins/FortCollinsThanksgivingDayRun)) or Boulder Kids 800M on Pearl St (9 am, ages 1–12, $22.85, [link](https://runsignup.com/Race/Info/CO/Boulder/AlpineBankBoulderThanksgivingDay5K)).
